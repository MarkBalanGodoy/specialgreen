## Send them bright. The site does the darkening.

Real photographs only. Bright, natural daylight, sharp, shot on actual Special Green
properties. No stock, no illustration, no AI imagery, and nothing that shows work the
company did not do.

The site applies the dark overlay, high contrast, vignette and warm grade itself. That is why
the source photos need to be bright and clean: a bright source under a dark treatment reads
cinematic, a dark source under a dark treatment reads muddy.

"Nothing washed out" means avoid flat midday haze and blown highlights, not avoid brightness.
Golden hour and bright overcast both work. Direct noon sun flattens turf.

Export at 2400px wide for full-bleed positions, 1600px for cards, JPEG quality 80, plus a
WebP alongside. Every hero-scale image needs a mobile crop at 1200x1500 vertical.

## Shooting notes

Shoot the crew on a normal workday, not a staged one. Clean shirts, but real work.

Golden hour for anything wide. Midday sun flattens turf and kills the contrast the design
depends on.

Get the orange in frame wherever a person appears. The orange shirt against dark green is the
single strongest brand asset on this site and the reason the dark direction works.

Shoot vertical versions of the hero, crew, and commercial shots at the same time. Most of the
traffic will be on a phone.
