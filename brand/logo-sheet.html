Rules

  • Clear space on every side is the height of the mark's ground rule. Nothing crowds it.
  • Minimum width for the horizontal lockup is 160px on screen, 1.25 inches in print. Below that, use the stacked version or the mark alone.
  • Do not recolour the leaves, restack the words, add a shadow, or set the badge on a busy photo. Over a photo, use the knockout on a darkened area.
  • The badge carries the phone number. The web lockup deliberately does not. If the number ever changes, only the badge needs reissuing.
  • The badge says LAWN CARE & LANDSCAPING. The company also sells irrigation, and the web lockup says so. Worth reconciling the two.
