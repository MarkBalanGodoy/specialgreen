Rules

  • Clear space on every side is the height of the mark's ground rule. Nothing crowds it.
  • Minimum width for the horizontal lockup is 160px on screen, 1.25 inches in print. Below that, use the stacked version or the mark alone.
  • Do not recolour the leaves, restack the words, add a shadow, outline it, or set it on a busy photo. On a photo, use the one-colour white version over a darkened area.
  • The orange rule is the only orange in the mark. It is what ties the logo to the crew shirts.
  • The service line is part of the logo. If it is too small to read, use the mark alone instead of shrinking the words.
