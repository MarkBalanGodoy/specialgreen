Yard Guides

Straight Answers
For Louisiana Yards.

Most lawn advice online is written for somewhere else. These are written for zone 9a: St. Augustine and centipede turf, heavy clay, and the humidity that causes half the problems we get called about.

Lawn DiseaseBrown Patch in St. Augustine.If circles of your lawn are going yellow and spreading outward in spring or fall, you are almost certainly looking at large patch, the disease most people around here call brown patch.FeedingWhen to Fertilize St. Augustine.Almost every fertilizing mistake we get called about is a timing mistake, not a product mistake. Here is the timing that matters in our climate.Lawn PestsChinch Bugs, Or Just Dry?Both look like a lawn drying out. One is solved with water, the other gets worse while you water it. Telling them apart takes about two minutes.Lawn PestsFall Armyworms. Speed Matters.Most lawn problems give you weeks to react. This one gives you days, and that is the only thing you really need to remember about it.DrainageWater That Will Not Drain.If water is still sitting in your yard a day after the rain stopped, no amount of fertilizer, sod or reseeding is going to fix it. That is a drainage job.MowingCut It Taller Than You Think.Most struggling St. Augustine lawns we look at are being cut too short. Raising the deck is free, takes one minute, and fixes more than most products do.WateringWater Deep, Water Early.Watering a little every day feels careful and produces a shallow-rooted lawn that cannot cope with the first hot week. Less often and deeper is the whole trick.
